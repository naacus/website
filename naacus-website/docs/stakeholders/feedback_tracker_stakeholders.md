## What Has Been Fixed ✅

### Website Look & Feel
- ✅ The website colors now reflect the **NAACUS logo** — royal blue, leafy green, and gold tones are used throughout the site, including the hero banner.

### Programs & Activities Page
- ✅ The word **"Biannual"** has been corrected to **"Biennial"** to match proper Catholic conference terminology.

### Membership Page
- ✅ The membership form now shows **"(Arch)Diocese"** instead of just "Diocese."
- ✅ A new **"Community / African Catholic Group"** field has been added to the form (e.g., Nigerian Catholic Community, Cameroon Catholic Community).
- ✅ The **"Years in the United States"** field is now clearly marked as **Optional** — no one is required to fill it in.
- ✅ **Membership dues are now clearly shown** at the top of the membership form before anyone fills it out:
  - Individual: **$20.00 per person**
  - Group (2–100 members): **$200.00 one-time**
  - Group (100+ members): **$300.00 one-time**

### 2025 Conference Page
- ✅ A **"View Photo Gallery"** button has been added linking to the official NAACUS 2025 conference photos by photographer Kwame Frimpong (sikapalens.com).

- ✅ The **full official Schedule of Events** is now displayed — all 28 sessions across Friday July 18, Saturday July 19, and Sunday July 20, with accurate times, presenters, locations, and parallel workshop tracks (English Adults, French Adults, Youth, Young Adults).
