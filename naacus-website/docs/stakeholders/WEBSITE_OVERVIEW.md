## What Is This Website?

The NAACUS website is the organization's official online presence. It serves as a central hub where members, visitors, and supporters can learn about NAACUS, attend events, join as members, make donations, access resources, and get in touch with the team.

The site is available in **English and French**, works on **desktops, tablets, and phones**, and includes an **AI-powered chatbot** to help visitors find information quickly.

---

## Website Pages at a Glance

The website has **17 pages**, all accessible from the top navigation bar or the footer. Here is what each page does:

### Home Page
The landing page visitors see first. It features:
- A **rotating hero banner** with key messages and calls to action
- A **Conference 2027 teaser** preview
- **Member benefits** highlights showing reasons to join
- **Testimonials** from community members
- A **photo gallery** showcasing the community
- A **newsletter sign-up** section
- A **contact** section

### About
Tells the story of NAACUS — who we are, our mission, and what we stand for.

### Leadership
Profiles of the organization's leadership team with photos and titles.

### Fellowship & Ministries
An overview of all fellowship groups and ministries within NAACUS. Visitors can click into any ministry to see detailed information about its mission and activities.

### Programs & Activities
A listing of ongoing programs and community activities members can participate in.

### NAACUS 2025
A dedicated page for the 2025 national conference, including event details, accomplishments, photos, testimonials, and videos.

### Events
A listing of all upcoming and past events, with featured event highlights.

### Membership
Information about membership plans and an online registration form. Three plans are available:

| Plan | Price | Who It's For |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | $20/year | Single members |
| **Group (2–100)** | $200 (one-time) | Small to mid-size organizations |
| **Group (100+)** | $300 (one-time) | Large organizations |

### Volunteer
A sign-up page for members who want to volunteer their time and talents.

### Donation
A page where supporters can make financial contributions. The website accepts payments through:
- Credit and debit cards (via Stripe)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Bank transfers
- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC)

### Resources
Downloadable documents, guides, and quick links useful for members.

### Newsletters
An archive of past newsletters so members can catch up on what they missed.

### Contact
A contact form for visitors to send messages to the NAACUS team.

### Feedback
A dedicated page where visitors can share feedback about the website or organization. This link is highlighted in red in the navigation to draw attention.

### FAQ
Frequently asked questions and answers about NAACUS and its services.

### Privacy Policy
Explains how NAACUS collects, uses, and protects visitor data.

## Features Available on Every Page

No matter which page a visitor is on, these features are always available:

| Feature | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Navigation Bar** (top of page) | Links to all major sections; collapses into a menu icon on phones |
| **Search** | A search box in the header to quickly find content across the site |
| **Language Switcher** | Toggle between English and French at any time |
| **Donate Button** | A quick-access donate button in the header |
| **Feedback Button** | A highlighted button linking to the feedback form |
| **AI Chat Assistant** | A chat bubble in the bottom-right corner; visitors can ask questions and get instant answers |
| **Cookie Consent Banner** | Appears on first visit asking permission to use cookies for analytics |
| **Back to Top Button** | A floating button that scrolls the visitor back to the top of the page |
| **Footer** | Contains quick links, engagement links, "Get Involved" section, privacy policy, and cookie settings |

## Language Support

The entire website is available in two languages:

- **English** (default)
- **French**

Visitors can switch languages at any time using the language toggle in the top-right corner. The site remembers their language preference for future visits.

---

## AI Chat Assistant

A chat assistant (powered by Microsoft Copilot Studio) is available on every page as a floating button in the bottom-right corner. Visitors can:

- Ask questions about NAACUS
- Get help finding information on the website
- Learn about events, membership, and programs

The assistant responds instantly with helpful answers, 24/7.

The website supports multiple ways to give:

| Payment Method | Description |
|---|---|

| **PayPal** | Pay with a PayPal account |

## Analytics & Tracking

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site. Analytics are only activated **after a visitor accepts cookies** via the consent banner. The data helps the team understand:

- Which pages are most visited
- How visitors navigate the site
- How far visitors scroll on each page
- Which buttons and links are most clicked

No personal data is shared with third parties. See the Privacy Policy page for details.

---

## Where the Website Is Hosted

The website is hosted on **Microsoft Azure Static Web Apps**, a cloud hosting service. This provides:

- Fast loading times worldwide
- Automatic security (HTTPS)
- High availability (the site stays online even under heavy traffic)

## Summary

The NAACUS website is a modern, bilingual (English/French), mobile-friendly website that allows the organization to:

- **Inform** visitors about NAACUS, its mission, leadership, and ministries
- **Engage** the community through events, programs, and newsletters
- **Grow** membership through online registration and multiple payment options
- **Support** fundraising through a flexible donation system
- **Assist** visitors instantly with an AI-powered chat assistant
- **Communicate** with visitors via contact and feedback forms
- **Share** resources, photos, videos, and testimonials from the community
