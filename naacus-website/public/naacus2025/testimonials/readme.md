# NAACUS 2025 Testimonials

This folder contains testimonial content from participants of the NAACUS 2025 gathering.

## Guidelines for Adding Testimonials

### Content Types
1. **Written Testimonials**: Text quotes from participants
2. **Video Testimonials**: Short video clips of participants sharing experiences
3. **Audio Testimonials**: Audio recordings
4. **Photo Testimonials**: Photos with quotes overlaid

### Written Testimonials Format

Create a `testimonials.json` file:

```json
{
  "testimonials": [
    {
      "id": "testimonial-001",
      "name": "Grace Okafor",
      "location": "Houston, TX",
      "role": "Parishioner, Sacred Heart Parish",
      "quote": "NAACUS 2025 renewed my faith and connected me with an amazing community. I felt truly at home celebrating my African heritage and Catholic faith together.",
      "photo": "grace-okafor.jpg",
      "date": "2025-07-18",
      "category": "participant"
    },
    {
      "id": "testimonial-002",
      "name": "Fr. Emmanuel Banda",
      "location": "Chicago, IL",
      "role": "Pastor, St. Augustine Parish",
      "quote": "The workshops on African Catholic evangelization provided practical tools I'm already implementing in my parish. This conference is transforming how we serve our community.",
      "photo": "fr-emmanuel-banda.jpg",
      "date": "2025-07-17",
      "category": "clergy"
    }
  ]
}
```

### Video Testimonial Specifications
- Length: 30-90 seconds ideal
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1080p or 720p
- Include name and location overlay
- Good audio quality essential

### Photo Guidelines
- Headshots of testimonial givers: 400x400px minimum
- Professional or candid quality
- Obtain written consent
- Include photo release forms

### Categories
- `participant`: Regular attendees
- `clergy`: Priests, deacons, religious
- `youth`: Young adults and students
- `organizer`: Conference organizers
- `speaker`: Keynote speakers and presenters
- `volunteer`: Event volunteers
- `first-timer`: First-time attendees

### Gathering Testimonials

**Questions to Ask:**
1. What was your favorite moment from NAACUS 2025?
2. How has this event impacted your faith journey?
3. What surprised you most about the gathering?
4. How do you plan to bring this experience back to your parish?
5. What would you tell someone considering attending NAACUS 2027?
6. How did celebrating African Catholic heritage enrich your experience?

### Consent & Permissions
- Obtain written consent for all testimonials
- Include release forms in `consent-forms/` subfolder
- Specify how testimonials will be used
- Allow participants to review before publishing

### Organization
Suggested folder structure:
```
testimonials/
├── written/
│   └── testimonials.json
├── video/
│   ├── testimonial-001.mp4
│   └── thumbnails/
├── audio/
│   └── testimonial-001.mp3
├── photos/

├── consent-forms/
└── readme.md
```

### Best Practices
- Diverse representation (age, region, background)
- Mix of clergy, religious, and laity
- Include youth voices
- Highlight different aspects (faith, fellowship, culture, learning)
- Keep quotes concise and impactful
- Proofread for grammar and clarity
- Verify names and titles

### Template for Collection Form

**Testimonial Submission Form:**
- Full Name*
- Email*
- Parish/Organization
- City, State*
- Role (Participant/Clergy/Youth/etc.)
- Your Testimonial* (200 words max)
- Photo Upload (optional)
- Video Upload (optional)
- Consent to publish* (checkbox)
- Preferred contact method

### Using Testimonials
- Rotate testimonials on website
- Feature in newsletter
- Share on social media
- Include in promotional materials for NAACUS 2027
